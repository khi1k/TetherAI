Project: Tether AI Technologies
Goal: Build a web app for small business owners (barber shops, corner stores) to track sales and manage customers.

Tech Stack:
- Backend: Python (Flask)
- Frontend: HTML/CSS/JS
- Database: SQLite
- Functional Language: Racket

Architecture Requirements:
1. Imperative: Sales processing, validation, loops.
2. Object-Oriented: Classes for Customer, Service, Visit, Business.
3. Functional: Racket for data transformations.
4. Declarative: SQL queries for reports.
5. AI Integration: Business insights engine.
